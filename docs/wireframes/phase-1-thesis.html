01/P1 · P2 · P4 together

The dashboard after Phase 1

Same data as the baseline — 19 inbox signals — reorganised. Note what the reclaimed 400px of dead space buys: full titles, a visible score on every row, and the sprint context that previously had nowhere to go.

  1. 1Score first, always. Mono numeral in the leading column, banded by the tier that already sorts the list. No unlabelled dot survives anywhere in the app.
  2. 2Group headers are obligations, not categories. "Waiting on you" encodes a debt; "GitHub" encodes a vendor.
  3. 3Source is a filter with a live count. It stays legible per row as the leading icon, which is where it was always doing useful work.
  4. 4Titles get ~78 characters before the ellipsis, against ~30 in the old columns. One line, always — row height is fixed per density mode, so a long title can never reflow the row. Anything clipped carries its full string in title and in the accessible name.
  5. 5Every group collapses after five with "Show N more". No scroll trap, no second column, no stretched empty card.
  6. 6The bottom "Everything else · 9" card is gone. It was the low band of the same list; it is now the fourth group, in rank order, with its threshold rule visible.
  7. 7Actions rest at low emphasis. Start and ⋯ sit quiet until hover or focus — drawn here in their resting state, which is why they look faint.

02/P1 · the flagship pixel

Why 60?

Activating the chip — click, Enter, or x on the focused row — opens the arithmetic. Every entry comes from scoreBreakdown(), so the popover cannot drift from the sort order without the property test failing.

Decision · the zero state carries the rules The proposal asks that a zero score still explain itself. Going one step further: the popover also names the rules that did not fire — no deadline, not stale yet, 4 days of 5 — and names the exemption keeping the row on screen. A user who reads this popover has learned the whole formula without opening Settings. That is the difference between a transparent tool and a tool with a transparency page.

Accessibility of the chip

  • P1+Chip is a real <button>, accessible name "Urgency 60 of 105, show breakdown"
  • P1+Popover is role="dialog" with a labelled heading; focus enters on open and returns to the chip on close
  • P1+Urgency is carried by the numeral, so 1.4.1 holds with colour removed (see the Phase 0 greyscale proof)
  • P1+Chip text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 in all four bands — dark ink on the two filled bands, not white

03/P2 · the one judgement call

Which group does a signal land in?

One pure function, total over the Reason union, with precedence resolved in one place. Blocked wins when it collides with Waiting on you, because the required action differs: a nudge, not work.

GroupReason / statePointsWhy it belongs here
BlockedADO state matches /block/any Precedence 1 — takes any signal, whatever else applies to it
Waiting on youreview_requested · mention · approved_unmerged40 · 40 · 45 Precedence 2 — a person is waiting for a reply, a review, or a merge
Moving without youstale_own_pr · authored30 · 10 Precedence 3 — your work, in someone else's queue. Time-sensitive, not actionable
Lower priorityassigned · manual · anything below 2510 Remainder. Collapsed, but exempt rows stay drawn
Deviation from the proposal The proposal files "your PR approved and mergeable" under Moving without you. That is wrong for this codebase. approved_unmerged is the highest-scoring reason in the whole formula at 45 — the repo already believes it is the most urgent thing that can happen to you, and it is urgent precisely because it is now waiting on you to merge. Filing it under "moving without you" would put the top-scoring reason in the informational group. It goes in Waiting on you. Ariadne can't merge it for you, but it can stop pretending the ball isn't in your court.
Deviation from the proposal The section is titled Signals · 19, not Needs attention · 10. Folding the old "Everything else" card in would otherwise make the headline count jump from 10 to 19 and read as new work arriving. "Signals · 19 / 12 need something from you" gives both numbers their own denominator, which §12 question 6 asks for anyway. "Lower priority" is also adopted here rather than in Phase 4 — renaming a label you created ten minutes ago is free.

04/P4 · status legend

A colour system users can decode

Badges now have one filled state, and it is the one that needs a human. A small legend sits behind a ? next to the group headers, because a colour system nobody can read is decoration.
